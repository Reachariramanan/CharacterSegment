Lines
    Controls
    • Click in a line: add boundary
    • Drag a red line: move boundary
    • Dbl-click / right-click a line: remove
    • Arrow keys: nudge selected (Shift = 5px)
    • N / P: next / previous line
    • Split line / New line pencil: click-and-drag to draw freehand (streams a dense trail of points for fine control), a plain click just adds one point and continues as before, hold Shift while dragging to draw a straight segment instead; Enter to confirm, Esc to cancel
    • New line: trace the top edge, Tab for the bottom edge, Enter to confirm, Esc to cancel
    • Extend line: select a line first -- retraces its top/bottom edges seeded with its current curve (same cyan/pink, Tab, Enter/Esc as New line), so adding points beyond either end grows the line further left/right instead of replacing it
    • Delete line: select a line, then Delete line (or press Delete/Backspace with no boundary selected)
    • Clear all annotation: wipes every line/boundary on this page (asks for confirmation first) -- unsaved until Save
    • Reset line: reset just the selected line back to its CV-assist proposal
    • Scissor: a short snip near a boundary deletes just that boundary; a wide drag near a line's edge trims that edge inward to the nearest boundary (cuts off scanner-noise margins without deleting the line). No confirmation, so double-check your drag; toggle off with the button or Esc
    • Show annotation: off by default (page loads blank) -- toggle on to see your line bands + boundaries
    • Show model output: blue dashed overlay = current model prediction (read-only preview, doesn't touch your annotation) -- line and akshara-boundary seams show as slightly curved, ink-avoiding cuts (computed directly, no training needed)
    • Adopt model output: copies the previewed model output into your editable annotation (unsaved -- Save to persist as a new version)
    • Page dropdown: every rendered page (data/images/) is listed; a ✓ marks pages that have a saved annotation. A page is not auto-initialized on load anymore -- a blank one shows just the image until you click Initialize CV proposals
    • Initialize CV proposals: bootstraps a blank page with fresh CV-assist proposals (unsaved -- Save to persist and make it training-eligible)
    • Train boundaries / Train lines: continues training from the current checkpoint (never restarts from scratch), on every page that has a saved annotation (not just the one open here) -- a full pass, progress shown top-right
    • Update model: same idea but a quicker, shorter pass on both models, also across every saved-annotation page -- good for a small touch-up after just a few more corrections
    • Train YOLO: fine-tunes YOLO26n-seg (a separate, pretrained instance-segmentation model) on two classes, "line" and per-akshara "character" -- starts from its COCO-pretrained weights the first time, then its own fine-tuned checkpoint after that. Check original/finetuned YOLO preview its raw output (orange/purple) -- read-only, no "adopt" (its polygon output doesn't map onto boundaries_px)
    • Scroll: zoom, Space+drag: pan
    • Ctrl+S: save
